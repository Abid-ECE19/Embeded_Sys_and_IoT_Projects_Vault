This is a residential solar system development project where I tried to implement my learnings on CAD designs, Excel proficiency and Electrical engineering knowledge in one place. I tried to keep things as close to professional cause it's a part of my learning that I believe will help me in my career.

As I'm not a professional, nor work in a Solar Specialist company; I don't have the exact business data, project structure and documentation demo on my hand. So I took help from Google & AI platforms to collect realistic end point data (customer requirements, solar specifications, installation area data etc.) which helped me to make this project stand.

I used NanoCAD for most of the 2D drawings, module placement etc. and SkyCAD for electrical schematics - as a free alternative to AutoCAD Electrical. And also used Excel for data management, calculations, BOM etc. I tried to maintain SREDA and IEC guidelines which in my knowledge, is the standard for BD industries.
