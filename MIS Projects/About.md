This folder holds my self-learned MIS projects, where I work with datasets to understand business problems, uncover useful insights, and support better decision-making. 

Each project follows a practical workflow: starting with understanding and preparing the data, followed by validation, SQL-based analysis, KPI development, dashboards, visualization, and business recommendations.

The projects I hope will cover different industries and business areas - sales, retail, inventory, marketing, finance, operations, and performance management. The tools I use for such tasks are generally MySQL Workbench, Excel, Power BI, DAX, and other analytical tools. 
My focus is not just on producing charts or writing queries, but to understand what the data is saying, why it matters from a business perspective, and how the findings can be communicated clearly.
