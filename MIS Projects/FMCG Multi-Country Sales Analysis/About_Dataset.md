# Dataset Character
The dataset contains one CSV file: “fmcg_sales_3years_1M_rows.csv” - a 3-year sales record (from 1-Jan-2021 to 31-Dec-2023) of 15 countries and 60+ cities, having 1.1M+ business records. With over 350+ SKU, seasonality patterns, promotions & segmentation data – this dataset is designed for real-life business cases, job interviews, portfolio projects, coursework, and professional S&OP simulations.
